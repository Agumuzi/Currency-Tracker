# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Agumuzi projects welcome constructive participation regardless of experience, background, identity, or technical skill. We aim to keep issues, discussions, reviews and contributions respectful, useful and accessible.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Provide reproducible reports and actionable, relevant feedback.
- Respect privacy, licenses, consent and intellectual property.
- Use inclusive language and welcome newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, publication of private data or credentials, deliberate disruption, and other abusive conduct are not permitted.

## Scope and moderation

This policy applies to repository Issues, Discussions, pull requests, code reviews and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove inappropriate content, close or lock threads, and restrict participation when necessary. Serious or repeated abuse may lead to a permanent ban.

For inappropriate GitHub content, use GitHub's built-in **Report content** mechanism. For ordinary, non-sensitive project moderation requests, open an Issue with a concise description. Do not publicly disclose private personal details or security vulnerabilities; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security reports.
